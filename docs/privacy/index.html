Blackjack Advantage Trainer Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 2, 2026

Blackjack Advantage Trainer is an educational blackjack simulation for Android and iOS, identified as com.blackjackadvantage.trainer. It works without an account and stores learning progress locally.

Your choices

A Firebase-enabled beta may ask separately for permission to collect anonymous usage analytics and technical crash reports. Both choices are off by default, declining does not limit the app, and either choice can be changed later in Progress.

Local information

The device stores the selected experience level, lesson scores and resume positions, XP and streak data, spaced-review state, guidance acknowledgements, and consent versions. Resetting progress or uninstalling the app removes the app's local copy, subject to operating-system backups.

Optional usage analytics

After opt-in, custom events may contain stable lesson or exercise IDs, experience level, session type, correctness, and aggregate results. Answer text and card sequences are never sent. Google Analytics for Firebase may also process an app-instance identifier and technical app, device, operating-system, approximate-region, and timing information.

Optional crash reports

After a separate opt-in, Firebase Crashlytics may process stack traces, crash state, app and operating-system versions, device model, and related diagnostics. The app does not intentionally attach gameplay content, contact information, or a custom user identifier.

What the app does not do

The beta has no ads, accounts, real-money wagering, remote gameplay history, camera or microphone recognition, location access, casino connection, or cross-app tracking. Data is not sold.

Retention and deletion

Disabling a telemetry choice stops future collection through the app. Previously processed Firebase data follows the project's Google retention controls. With no account or custom user identifier, an individual remote diagnostic record may not be linkable to a deletion request.

Platform services

Apple, Google, device manufacturers, app stores, TestFlight, Firebase App Distribution, and operating-system backups may independently process technical or account information under their own policies.
